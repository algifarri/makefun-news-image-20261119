One image that sums up the world news of **Thursday 19 November 2026**, drawn
procedurally by `main.py` — a pure standard-library Python program with a
hand-rolled framebuffer, a 5x7 bitmap font and a manual PNG encoder. No
network, no randomness: every run writes the same bytes.

The poster is a newspaper front page. The hero story is Grand Theft Auto VI
launching worldwide after a 13-year wait — Vice City opens at midnight in
each region, with a 99 on Metacritic and about $1.5 billion in preorders.
Four panels cover the first-ever Trump–Xi–Putin trilateral meeting in
Shenzhen at the APEC summit, the US and Iran exchanging fire again as Iran
widens its attacks on Gulf states, the International Court of Justice
ruling in the genocide case over the Rohingya in Myanmar, and NASA's
Voyager 1 reaching one light-day from Earth almost 50 years after launch.
A ticker along the bottom adds the EU's planned €45bn loan for Ukraine,
Canada adjusting its China ties, and Australia's sanctions on Israeli
settlers in the West Bank.

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out python3 main.py
```

This writes `out/entry.png` (1024x1024 PNG, about 19 KB).
